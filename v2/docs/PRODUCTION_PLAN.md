# Version 2 — production plan (all 17 scenes)

Narration text is the author's, unchanged (Version 1's screenplay and Kokoro narration are reused for timing). This plan covers only filmmaking: staging, camera, light, animation. Shot counts are what the beats need, not padding.

**Visual arc.** Waking world: warm late-autumn naturalism, golden and blue-hour light, handheld-feeling low cameras at Orion's eye height. Night: practical lamps, moonlight through the window. Dreams: the same world, but light gets freer — first heavy and amber (honey), then cool and weightless, and finally, at scene 15, the camera itself is unlocked and flies.

**Recurring assets.** Orion (one rig, one costume system: day clothes / pajamas / pajamas + cape), Mom, Dad, the driver, chickens; sets: yard (built), garage, kitchen + stairs, bedroom, coop, dream sky. Red sneakers and the telephone wires appear in every yard shot as quiet setup for scene 16.
