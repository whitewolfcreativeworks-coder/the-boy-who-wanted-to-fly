# The Boy Who Wanted to Fly
### An animated short — screenplay for Version 4
*Adapted for animation from the story by Jason Whitewolf (© 2018). Draft 1.*

---

## How this adaptation works

The book tells. The film **shows**.

- **The narrator** becomes a warm storyteller who speaks rarely: to open the film, to bridge a jump in time, and to deliver the ending. About one line in five of the book's narration survives.
- **The characters** speak in short, natural lines. Where the book's words are already perfect, they're kept exactly: "If I'm going to fly, I need to make some wings!" · "Here goes nothing!" · "More than anything." · "Practice, my boy. Practice." · "I think you can." · "Maybe it's my heart…"
- **Everything else is action, music and sound.** Wordless sequences carry the running, the failing, the chicken, the leaf pile and the flight.
- **The meaning is untouched.** A boy who won't give up; kind grown-ups who believe in him; the discovery that it's his heart that lets him fly; and the shoes on the telephone wire.
- **"Superman"** in the dialogue becomes "superheroes". The meaning is the same, there's no trademark in the film, and it stays true to a kid copying the heroes on his wall.

`[shot ids]` refer to the approved key images in `v3/keyframes/`. **NEW** marks a shot to be generated.
Target running time: about **8–9 minutes**, compared with 12 for Version 3.

## 1 · THE DREAM
`[s01a] [s01b]`

**EXT. ORION'S STREET — MORNING**
*A crisp autumn morning. Maples on fire with color. A flock of birds sweeps over the rooftops of a pale-yellow house with a red door, and over the telephone wires.*

**NARRATOR**
> Orion had one big dream.

*On the lawn, ORION (7) lies on his back in the leaves, arms spread wide, watching the birds wheel overhead. He flaps his arms, slowly, matching their wingbeats.*

**NARRATOR**
> He wanted to fly.

*MUSIC: a light, curious theme, plucked strings and a small whistle melody. This is Orion's theme; it comes back in every scene.*

## 2 · THE WORKSHOP
`[s01c] [s01d] [s01e]`

**INT. GARAGE — DAY**
*Dusty sunbeams. Orion digs through boxes, holds up a flattened cardboard box, and his face lights up.*

**ORION**
> If I'm going to fly… I need to make some wings!

*MONTAGE, cut to the music: a pencil sketches a wing shape; scissors chew through cardboard; tape SHRIEKS off the roll; a lopsided first wing; a better second one; tape wound around small arms.*

*Orion turns to an old mirror and spreads his arms. Two magnificent cardboard wings. He beams.*

## 3 · FLAP, FLAP, FLAP
`[s02a] [s02b] [s02c] [s02d]`

**EXT. FRONT YARD — LATE AFTERNOON**
*Orion charges across the lawn flapping with everything he has. Leaves explode under his sneakers. He leaps, lands, leaps higher, lands harder.*

*Wordless. MUSIC builds hopefully, then stumbles comically on every landing.*

*Finally he flops down in the grass, out of breath. The sun sinks low and gold. A V of geese passes overhead, effortless, honking softly.*

*Orion watches them go. A small, wistful look.*

*Wood smoke curls from chimneys. Pumpkins glow on porches.*

## 4 · DINNER
`[s02e] [s02f] [s03a]`

**EXT. FRONT PORCH — DUSK**

**MOM** *(calling, warm)*
> Orion! Dinner!

**INT. KITCHEN — NIGHT**
*Spaghetti and meatballs, his favorite. Orion slurps one long noodle; Mom and Dad laugh. Later, all three at the sink, washing up. Soap bubbles drift up.*

**DAD**
> Any luck today, pilot?

**ORION** *(mouth full, determined)*
> Not yet.

## 5 · A ROOM FULL OF FLYING THINGS
`[s03b] [s03c] [s03d] [s03e]`

**INT. STAIRS / ORION'S ROOM — NIGHT**
*Orion climbs the stairs. His room is a museum of flight: airplane posters, bird drawings, a hot-air balloon, a caped kid hero with a star on his chest, a model plane turning slowly on its string.*

*He touches the model plane, sets it spinning, and watches it "fly."*

*Toothbrush. Pajamas. Bed. Moonlight. He falls asleep smiling.*

*MUSIC: Orion's theme, now as a soft lullaby on music box.*

## 6 · FEATHERS
`[s04a] [s04b] [s04c] [s04d] [s04e] [s05a] [s05b] [s05c]`

**INT. ORION'S ROOM — MORNING**
*Orion's eyes pop open. He sits bolt upright.*

**ORION**
> Feathers!

**INT. STAIRS — CONTINUOUS**
*He thunders down the stairs.*

**MOM** *(from the kitchen, without turning around)*
> No running on the stairs.

*Orion freezes mid-stride. Then walks the last three steps with exaggerated, tiptoe dignity.*

**ORION**
> …Yes, Mom.

**INT. KITCHEN — MORNING**
*A tall stack of blueberry pancakes lands in front of him.*

**MOM**
> Pancakes first.

**ORION**
> Oh, man.

*He eats at record speed, cheeks bulging, eyes on the back door. Plate clean, he's gone in a blur. The chair wobbles behind him. A beat. He darts back, rights the chair, and vanishes again. Mom smiles into her coffee.*

**EXT. CHICKEN COOP — MORNING**
*Orion tiptoes among the hens, collecting feathers. One cheeky brown HEN has taken a professional interest in his red sneakers: peck, peck.*

**ORION** *(giggling)*
> They're not food!

*CUT TO: the back steps. Orion tapes feathers onto his wings, totally absorbed. Behind him the hen has the hem of his shirt in her beak, tugging with great determination. He doesn't notice.*

*He holds up the finished wings, half cardboard, half chicken.*

## 7 · HERE GOES NOTHING
`[s06a] [s06b] [s06c]`

**EXT. FRONT YARD — MIDDAY**
*Orion squares up at the end of the lawn like an athlete.*

**ORION**
> Here goes nothing!

*He runs. Flaps. Feathers fly off him like a molting bird. No liftoff. He keeps going until his legs give out and he collapses on the grass, a few feathers drifting down around him.*

*Silence. Then a thought arrives. He sits up, finger raised.*

**ORION**
> Higher. I just need to jump higher.

*INT. GARAGE: he wrestles a tall ladder out of the clutter, then grabs a rake.*

## 8 · THE LEAF PILE
`[s07a] [s07b] [s07c] [s07d]`

**EXT. FRONT YARD — AFTERNOON**
*The maples glow red, orange, pink and gold. Orion rakes the fallen leaves into an enormous, fluffy mountain.*

*He climbs the ladder, carefully, one rung at a time. At the top he spreads his feathered wings against the blue sky.*

*MUSIC holds its breath.*

*He jumps.*

*For one heartbeat he hangs there. Then he drops straight into the pile. WHUMP. Leaves EXPLODE into the air.*

*Beat. A small laugh from inside the pile. Then a big one.*

*MONTAGE: climb, jump, WHUMP, laugh. Again. Again. He isn't flying, but he's having the time of his life.*

## 9 · THE DRIVER
`[s08a] [s08b] [s08c] [s08d] [s08e]`

**EXT. ORION'S STREET — LATE AFTERNOON**
*A green garbage truck rumbles up and hisses to a stop. The DRIVER climbs down: big, burly, stubbly, enormous boots. He looks like trouble. Then he smiles, and he doesn't.*

*He walks over and kneels beside the leaf pile, where Orion sits covered in leaves and feathers.*

**DRIVER**
> You really want to learn to fly?

**ORION**
> More than anything.

*The driver chuckles, a deep warm rumble.*

**DRIVER**
> Then here's a secret. You can fly in your dreams.

**ORION** *(wide-eyed)*
> How?

**DRIVER** *(a thumbs-up, eyes crinkling)*
> Practice, my boy. Practice. And never give up.

*He climbs back into the truck. Two cheerful honks. The truck rumbles away.*

*Orion lies back in the leaves and looks up at the sky. He's thinking about it. He's decided.*

## 10 · GOODNIGHT
`[s09a] [s09b] [s09c]`

**INT. ORION'S ROOM — NIGHT**
*Quilt pulled up to his chin. Eyes wide open. Mom and Dad come in quietly. Mom kisses his forehead, her long hair brushing his face, and he scrunches up, smiling.*

**ORION**
> Dad? Do you think I can fly in my dreams?

*Dad considers it, seriously, the way you answer a real question.*

**DAD**
> I think you can.

*Orion smiles and burrows deep under the quilt. Eyes close.*

## 11 · RUNNING IN HONEY
`[s10a] [s10b] [s10c]`

**EXT. WINDOW — NIGHT**
*Wind. A bare branch taps the glass: tap… tap… tap…*

**DREAM: A GOLDEN PLACE**
*Orion is running, or trying to. The ground is thick, glowing honey. Every step is slow and stretchy, each foot pulled free with a sticky SHLUP. He flaps. Nothing. The harder he tries, the slower he goes.*

*MUSIC: his theme, slowed and syrupy, comically low.*

*He gives up, and the honey melts into…*

**DREAM: THE SWIMMING POOL — SUMMER**
*Sunlight. Splashing. Laughter. Orion and his friends in a sparkling pool. A happy memory, a little hazy at the edges.*

## 12 · THE CAPE
`[s11a] [s11b]`

**INT. ORION'S ROOM — MORNING**
*Orion lies in bed, hands behind his head, working it out.*

**ORION** *(to himself)*
> Too heavy to run… couldn't even jump…

*He looks at the poster of the caped hero. Slow smile.*

**INT. ORION'S ROOM — NIGHT**
*Pajamas. A shiny red cape tied at the neck. Fists on hips. A heroic pose on the bed, for an audience of one stuffed bear.*

*He switches off the lamp.*

## 13 · LIKE A BUBBLE
`[s12a] [s12b]`

**DREAM: A VIOLET SKY**
*Soft peach clouds. Orion in his pajamas and cape. He crouches, gathers everything he has, and jumps.*

*And he doesn't come down.*

*He hangs in the air, astonished. Looks at his feet. Looks at the clouds. Then a delighted laugh. He's floating like a soap bubble, bobbing gently, the cape drifting.*

*MUSIC: his theme, for the first time, played full and bright.*

## 14 · HANDS, THUMBS AND THE HEART
`[s13a] [s13b] [s14a] [s14b]`

*He tries to go somewhere. He can't. He wobbles. He thinks of the heroes on his wall and stretches his arms out in front of him.*

**ORION**
> Superheroes fly with their hands!

*Better, sort of. He weaves like a kid on a new bike.*

**ORION**
> …Thumbs?

*He sticks out a thumb and ZIPS sideways like a shot. The other thumb: ZIP, back the other way. Thumb: ZIP. Thumb: ZIP. He comes to a dizzy stop.*

**ORION**
> Nope. Not thumbs.

*He hangs there, thinking. Then, like the hero on the poster, he puffs out his chest.*

*He glides forward. Smooth. Push the chest further, faster; pull back, slower; lean, and he banks into a beautiful turn. He laughs out loud.*

*Then he stops. Puzzled. He looks down at his pajama top. No star. No emblem. Nothing there.*

**ORION** *(quietly)*
> But I don't have anything on my chest…

*He puts his hand over his heart. Under his palm, something glows warm and golden.*

**ORION**
> Maybe… it's my heart.

*MUSIC: the theme swells.*

## 15 · OVER THE MOON
`[s15a] [s15b] [s15c] [s15d] [s15e]`

*The film's showpiece. Wordless.*

*Orion throws his arms wide open, and the glow in his chest blazes. He shoots upward, laughing.*

*Over his own neighborhood: his roof, the maples, the yellow house, the telephone wires, birds flying alongside him like old friends.*

*Up through pink and gold clouds.*

*Across the face of an enormous moon, a tiny caped silhouette.*

*Into the stars, past a swirling galaxy, faster, and faster, and faster…*

*MUSIC: the full orchestra, every version of Orion's theme from the film at once.*

## 16 · THE SHOES
`[s16a] [s16b] [s16c] [s16d]`

*Close on two small feet in red sneakers, laces flapping loose in the rushing wind. One shoe slips. Then the other.*

*They tumble away, down, down, through the dawn sky, toward a quiet street… and catch, laces tangled, on a telephone wire in front of a pale-yellow house with a red door.*

*They swing gently. Silence, except for birds.*

**NARRATOR**
> So if you ever see a pair of shoes hanging from a telephone wire…

*Pull back down the street. On every wire along the block hang more sneakers: blue, green, pink, high-tops and tiny ones, swaying in the morning light.*

**NARRATOR**
> …now you know. They belong to kids who were flying in their dreams.

## 17 · THE BOY WHO COULD FLY
`[s17a] [s17b]`

*DREAM MONTAGE: Orion soaring past floating islands and waterfalls in the sky, looping with the birds.*

**NARRATOR**
> From then on, Orion flew every night.

**EXT. FRONT YARD — MORNING**
*Orion walks out onto the lawn in his socks. He looks up at the wire.*

*His red sneakers sway there in the breeze.*

*He grins. A knowing, secret grin.*

**NARRATOR**
> He'd learned the secret. Never give up — and let your heart lead the way.

*The birds lift off the wire and wheel into the sky. Orion's theme, one last time, bright and simple.*

## Notes for the author

- **What changed most.** About 80% of the narration is gone. It is replaced by action the audience watches, short dialogue and music cues. The book's descriptive passages (the scent of wood smoke and apples, the colors of the leaves, the plush blankets) became pictures and sound instead of words.
- **New small moments, invented for the film.** Each is there to make the characters feel alive. All are yours to keep or cut:
  - Dad's "Any luck today, pilot?"
  - Orion coming back to straighten his chair.
  - "They're not food!"
  - The heroic pose for the stuffed bear.
  - "Too heavy to run… couldn't even jump…"
- **Kept word for word**, because they're perfect:
  - "If I'm going to fly, I need to make some wings!"
  - "Here goes nothing!"
  - "More than anything."
  - "Practice, my boy. Practice."
  - "Hey Dad, do you think I can fly in my dreams?", lightly trimmed.
  - "I think you can."
  - "Oh, man."
  - "Maybe… it's my heart."
- **The ending narration** keeps your meaning and nearly your words. It's the story's payoff, so the narrator saves his voice for it.
- **The final line** ("Never give up — and let your heart lead the way") is new. It ties the driver's advice to the heart discovery. Cut it if you'd rather end on the shoes.
