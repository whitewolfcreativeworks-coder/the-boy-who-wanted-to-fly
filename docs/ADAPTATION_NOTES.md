# Adaptation notes

The canonical manuscript (`manuscript/The_Boy_Who_Wanted_to_Fly_ORIGINAL.txt`) is untouched. The screenplay (`script/screenplay.py`) follows it scene for scene — all 17 scenes are present in order. Changes are limited to pacing and continuity:

| Scene | Change | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "this summer had been no exception" → "this autumn was no exception" | The story is set in late autumn throughout; avoids a continuity bump for young viewers. |
| 2 | Mom's off-screen call becomes a short spoken line ("Orion! Dinner time!") | Gives Mom a voice and a moment on screen. |
| 2 | "flapping his arms and jumping up in the air" trimmed from one sentence | Removes a near-repeat so narration doesn't drag. |
| 3 | "couldn't help but feel a sense of excitement and anticipation" trimmed | Pacing; the bedroom visuals carry the feeling. |
| 4–6, 8, 9, 11, 13, 14 | Quoted speech is voiced by the characters (Orion, Mom, Dad, the driver); narration keeps the surrounding text | "Narration and selected character dialogue" per the brief. |
| 7 | Leaf colors read as a list ("Red, orange, pink, yellow, and brown.") | Rhythm for read-aloud. |
| 8 | Stray quotation marks in the manuscript cleaned; "Practice, my boy, practice" split into two beats | Natural delivery. |
| 9 | Dad's question is addressed directly ("Hey Dad, do you think…") as written; footstep/carpet detail moved into the visuals | Pacing. |
| 10 | Pool memory staged as a warm, hazy flashback | Distinguishes memory from dream. |
| 13 | "his body would abruptly and sharply fly in that direction" → "zip sharply" | Read-aloud pacing; animated as comic zips with motion trails. |
| 14 | Final line split so "Maybe… it's my heart that makes me fly!" lands on its own beat | The emotional turning point the brief asked to emphasize. |
| 15 | The flight is cut into its stages (neighborhood → clouds → over the moon → stars → faster) | Each phrase gets its own shot. |
| 16 | The ending explanation is split across a close-up, a fall, the snag on the wire, and a pull-back revealing many shoes on wires | Makes the ending clear and memorable. |
| 17 | Adds a "memory pages" montage (wings, leaves, practice) under "he remembered the long journey" | Visual callback; no narration change. |

## Visual choices touching third-party IP
- The manuscript's Superman references are kept in the words but **not** in the images: Orion's cape is plain red, his chest shows star-print pajamas (no letter or shield), and the bedroom "superhero" poster is an original caped kid with a star.

## Safety staging
- The ladder jump lands in a deep, fluffy leaf pile, played for laughs; the climb is slow and careful.
- No running down the stairs is shown being corrected, as in the text.
